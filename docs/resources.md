# Resources: Further Reading & Tools for BCI Neuromorphic Engineers

## Landmark Papers

### Neuromorphic Computing
- Mead (1990) — "Neuromorphic electronic systems" (Proc. IEEE) — foundational neuromorphic computing paper
- Mahowald & Douglas (1991) — "A silicon neuron" (Nature)
- Merolla et al. (2014) — "A million spiking-neuron integrated circuit with a scalable communication network" (Science) — IBM TrueNorth
- Davies et al. (2018) — "Loihi: A neuromorphic manycore processor with on-chip learning" (IEEE Micro)

### SNN Training
- Bohte, Kok & La Poutré (2002) — "Error-backpropagation in temporally encoded networks of spiking neurons" — early SNN training
- Neftci, Mostafa & Zenke (2019) — "Surrogate Gradient Learning in Spiking Neural Networks" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine)
- Rueckauer et al. (2017) — "Conversion of Continuous-Valued Deep Networks to Efficient Event-Driven Networks" (Frontiers in Neuroscience) — ANN-to-SNN conversion

### BCI Engineering
- Hochberg et al. (2012) — "Reach and grasp by people with tetraplegia using a neurally controlled robotic arm" (Nature) — landmark BrainGate clinical paper
- Shenoy, Sahani & Churchland (2013) — "Cortical control of arm movements: a dynamical systems perspective" (Annual Review of Neuroscience)
- Simeral et al. (2021) — "Home use of a percutaneous wireless intracortical BCI" (Journal of Neural Engineering)

### Implantable Circuit Design
- Muller et al. (2015) — "A minimally invasive 64-channel wireless μECoG implant" (IEEE JSSC)
- Angotzi et al. (2019) — "SiNAPS: An implantable active pixel sensor CMOS-probe for simultaneous large-scale neural recordings" (Biosensors and Bioelectronics)

## Regulatory Standards
- **IEC 62304:2006+A1:2015** — Medical device software lifecycle processes
- **ISO 14971:2019** — Application of risk management to medical devices
- **ISO 10993 series** — Biological evaluation of medical devices
- **FDA guidance on implantable BCI devices and AI/ML-based software**
